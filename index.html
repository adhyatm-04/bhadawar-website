★ AGRA HERITAGE · SINCE 1963

Serving Agra with love for over 60 years.

Founded in 1963 at Mantola, Agra, Bhadawar Hotel & Foods has been a cornerstone of authentic North Indian cuisine, welcoming generations of families, travelers, and food lovers.

From our legendary slow-cooked Bhadawar Makhani to fresh tandoori breads, every recipe preserves the cherished balance of rich spices, pure vegetarian ingredients, and homestyle hospitality.
